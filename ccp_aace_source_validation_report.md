## 13. Source Conflicts

**One genuine source conflict was found this revision, and resolved via the established authority hierarchy — the first actual instance in the entire pass.**

- **LEVEL OF EFFORT (LOE) — S&K6 Ch.14 vs. RP 10S-90.** S&K6 Ch.14's text states: "It is generally characterized by a uniform rate of activity over a specific period of time for EV calculations on LOE activities; the earned value equals the actual costs" — read literally, this means EV=AC for LOE, so Cost Variance (not Schedule Variance) would always be zero. This is also the universally-standard EVM/ANSI-748 treatment of LOE. Per the authority hierarchy (RP 10S-90 governs terminology and supersedes S&K6 wording where they conflict), **RP 10S-90 governs**: question 14-26's claim that LOE's SV is "always zero" is confirmed correct. No app change was made or needed — this resolves the conflict in the app's favor.
